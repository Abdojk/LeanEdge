Approach

Diagnose. Improve. Sustain.

A simple, structured path built on DMAIC: Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, Control.

  1. Define & Measure

    1 Diagnose

    Map the process as it really runs, measure where time, errors and rework build up, and agree a clear problem statement and baseline.

  2. Analyze & Improve

    2 Improve

    Find the root causes with data, then redesign the workflow, reports or ERP steps to remove them, tested with the people who do the work.

  3. Control

    3 Sustain

    Put controls, KPIs and simple standard work in place, and hand over cleanly so the gains hold after the engagement ends.
